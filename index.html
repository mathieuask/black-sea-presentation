Six nations. One sea.

Risk 07 // Resource Manipulation

Energy as a weapon

TurkStream (31.5 bcm/year) became Europe's sole Russian gas pipeline after Ukraine transit ended Dec 31, 2024. Operating at 98% capacity in Feb 2025.

March 2026: 12 drone attacks in two weeks on TurkStream compressor stations. If disrupted, European gas prices could spike 30-50% within days.

European gas storage: 22-27% (vs. 41% five-year average)

Risk 07 // Food Security

The grain blockade

Russia and Ukraine supply 34% of global wheat exports. The 2022 blockade trapped 25M tonnes and pushed 47M people into severe hunger.

The Black Sea Grain Initiative (Jul 2022 - Jul 2023) enabled 33M tonnes of exports before Russia withdrew. Ukraine's unilateral corridor has since shipped 90M+ tonnes.

Wheat prices surged 58% in March 2022. The FAO Food Price Index hit its all-time high.

Part VI

The UAMR Framework

Four steps to anticipate and manage geopolitical risk.

Understand

Define risk appetite. Romania/Bulgaria = stable. Turkey = volatile. Ukraine = high risk. Make GPR everyone's business.

Analyse

Combine GPR Index, insurance data, TurkStream flows with think-tank intelligence. Use scenario planning. Avoid groupthink.

Mitigate

Diversify supply chains. Invest in Constanta/Danube alternatives. Obtain MIGA insurance. Build flexible surge capacity.

Respond

Plan for failure. Activate crisis teams. Capitalize on near misses. Train continuously. Reward courageous decisions.

BCG & WEF (2026): fewer than 1 in 5 companies have a dedicated geopolitics department.

Conclusion

Compounding risk,
not a single threat

A drone strike on TurkStream (Type 9) triggers an energy spike (Type 7), which raises inflation, which pressures governments on sanctions (Type 3), which reshapes compliance obligations (Type 6).

In a world of compounding geopolitical risk, optionality is the most valuable corporate asset.
